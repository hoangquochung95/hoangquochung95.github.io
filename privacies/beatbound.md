# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Beatbound** mobile application (the “App”) handles information. It reflects the App’s current features and may need to be updated if those features or services change.

## Information the App handles

The App does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or other personal information. It does not include advertising or analytics functionality in its current version.

While you play, the App processes game information such as your selected song, score, hits, misses, and accuracy in memory so it can run the game and show your results. Settings such as volume and difficulty are also held in memory. The current version does not save this game or settings information to an account or send it to the developer; it is generally cleared when the App session ends.

## Song downloads and network requests

Some songs are included with the App. Other songs may be downloaded when you choose to play them. These downloads are served from `hoangquochung95.github.io`. On Android, downloaded audio may be kept in the App’s local storage for later playback. On the web, audio is requested from the hosting server for playback.

When your device requests a song, the hosting provider may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, the requested song file, and request time, and may process this information in accordance with its own policies. The App does not send your score or profile as part of the song download request. Please review the hosting provider’s privacy information for details about its handling of server requests.

## Device permissions and audio

The App plays music and may use Android audio-related system capabilities to support playback. Although the Android package currently declares a microphone permission, the current App does not record, access, or transmit microphone audio. It does not need microphone audio for gameplay.

## Storage and retention

Gameplay results and settings are kept in application memory and are not retained by the App after the session ends. Downloaded song files may remain in the App’s private device storage to avoid downloading them again. You can remove these files by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App. Browser-managed caching may apply when using the web version.

## Sharing and disclosure

The developer does not sell personal information. The App does not otherwise transmit gameplay data to the developer. Song download requests go to the hosting provider described above, which may process connection data to deliver and protect its service.

## Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to the developer in connection with the App, please contact us using the details below so the matter can be reviewed.

## Security

The App minimizes the information it handles, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Song downloads use HTTPS where supported by the hosting service. Please keep your device and operating system up to date.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as the App changes. The “Last updated” date above indicates when it was most recently revised. Material changes should be accompanied by an updated policy in the App’s distribution listing or another appropriate location.
